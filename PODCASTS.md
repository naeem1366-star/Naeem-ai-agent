# Supra AI Podcasts

The Podcasts space reads public RSS podcast feeds and streams the enclosure audio directly from the publisher's URL. It does not proxy, re-host, or bundle episode audio.

## Included public feeds

- BBC Global News Podcast: https://podcasts.files.bbci.co.uk/p02nq0gn.rss
- BBC 6 Minute English: https://podcasts.files.bbci.co.uk/p02pc9tn.rss
- BBC The Inquiry: https://podcasts.files.bbci.co.uk/p029399x.rss
- BBC The Infinite Monkey Cage: https://podcasts.files.bbci.co.uk/b00snr0w.rss
- NASA JPL Podcasts: https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/feeds/podcasts/
- Radio Raah (Persian): https://feed.podbean.com/RadioRaah/feed.xml
- Rahe Goosh (Persian): https://feeds.acast.com/public/shows/627aaa14f8074c0012c28528

Feeds are publicly accessible at the time they are added; availability, geographic restrictions, and episode rights remain controlled by each publisher. Public RSS access does not mean the audio is public domain.

Users can add their own HTTPS RSS feed. The app stores feed subscriptions and saved episode metadata locally. Audio is streamed, not downloaded for offline playback.

## Current playback scope

Playback uses Android MediaPlayer while the Podcasts screen is open. Background playback, notification/lock-screen controls, persistent resume position, playback speed, and sleep timer are follow-up enhancements.
